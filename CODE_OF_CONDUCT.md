# Code of Conduct

Warden CLI aims to be a practical and respectful project for people building safer local MCP workflows.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, constructive, and respectful.
- Assume good intent while still being precise about technical issues.
- Keep discussions focused on the work.
- Give and receive feedback with concrete examples.
- Respect maintainers' time by providing reproduction steps, context, and verification details.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publicly sharing private information without consent.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.
- Bad-faith vulnerability disclosure or exploit publication against users.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers through the repository's preferred private channel. If no private channel is configured yet, open an issue with minimal public detail and ask for a maintainer contact path.

Reports should include:

- What happened.
- Where it happened.
- Who was involved.
- Any relevant links or screenshots.
- Whether immediate action is needed.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block participants when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

Enforcement should be proportional, documented when practical, and focused on keeping the project safe and productive.
